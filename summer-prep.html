Newton's 3rd law reminder
The block pushes down on the surface with force equal to its weight. The surface pushes back up with the normal force. These are an action-reaction pair — equal in magnitude, opposite in direction, acting on different objects.

Why this matters
Energy methods often solve problems kinematics cannot — like finding the speed at the bottom of a curved ramp. You don't need to know the shape of the path. Just set PE_top = KE_bottom.

Dimensional analysis is a powerful error-checking tool. If the units on both sides of an equation don't match, the equation is wrong — guaranteed. Click any equation to see the unit check.

SI base units used in AP Physics 1:
m = meters (length) | kg = kilograms (mass) | s = seconds (time)
N = kg·m/s² | J = kg·m²/s² | W = kg·m²/s³
Exam strategy
If you forget a formula, dimensional analysis can often reconstruct it. Need an equation for distance from speed and time? Only v·t gives meters — so d = v·t must be right.
