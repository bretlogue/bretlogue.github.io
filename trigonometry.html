The Right Triangle Foundation

Every trig function starts with a right triangle. Label the angle you care about θ (theta). The three sides have names based on their relationship to θ.

AP Physics 1 connection
A force F at angle θ splits into two components: F·cos(θ) horizontal and F·sin(θ) vertical. This decomposition is essential for analyzing inclined planes, projectile motion, and equilibrium problems.

Special Angles You Must Memorize

These five angles appear constantly in AP Physics 1. Knowing them cold saves time on exams.

Memory trick: sin goes 0 → 1 as the angle grows; cos does the reverse. The pattern is √0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2.

Deriving Key Identities from the Unit Circle

Because any point on the unit circle satisfies x² + y² = 1, and since x = cos θ and y = sin θ, we get the most fundamental identity in trigonometry — directly from the Pythagorean theorem.

Pythagorean (core)
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Tangent definition
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
Derived from core
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
Derived from core
cot²θ + 1 = csc²θ
Complementary angles
sin θ = cos(90°−θ)
Symmetry (even/odd)
cos(−θ) = cos θ
sin(−θ) = −sin θ
AP Physics 1 — why this matters
When you find vector components using F·sin(θ) and F·cos(θ), the Pythagorean identity guarantees that the total magnitude is always F — no matter the angle. It's the mathematical proof that decomposing then recombining a vector gives you back what you started with.
